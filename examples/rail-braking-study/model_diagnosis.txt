I inspected the actual controller, robot model, and all three authoritative scenarios, then ran and verified simulations.

### Cause
The collision is caused by the controller’s fixed braking trigger:

- `robot/controller.py` brakes only when `wall_clearance <= 0.12 m`
- It ignores:
  - robot mass
  - current speed
  - actuator braking authority
  - required safety clearance
  - discrete-time reaction effects

### Evidence
Baseline controller failed in all scenarios:

- **A**: brakes at `0.12 m`, but physics predicts it needs about **0.36 m** to stop from `1.2 m/s` with `2.0 kg` and `4 N` braking.  
  Result: wall contact at `0.97 m/s`.
- **B**: heavier robot (`3.0 kg`) needs about **0.54 m** to stop.  
  Result: wall contact at `1.05 m/s`.
- **C**: changed wall location and initial speed; still needs about **0.25 m** to stop.  
  Result: wall contact at `0.71 m/s`.

Those are measured simulation results, independently verified.

### Repair proposed
I submitted a controller-only patch that:

- computes braking onset from **measured stopping distance**
- uses actual:
  - carriage mass
  - actuator force limits
  - timestep
  - current velocity
- includes a **reaction buffer**
- keeps braking latched near the wall so it doesn’t release too early
- actively damps residual reverse motion so final speed settles below the limit

### Verification result
The second proposal was independently executed and **passed A/B/C**:

- **Candidate:** `candidate_9ba053aaee684edf8f1914731a5449fc`

Measured outcomes:

- **A**: min clearance `0.0524 m`, final speed ~`0`
- **B**: min clearance `0.05237 m`, final speed ~`0`
- **C**: min clearance `0.05255 m`, final speed ~`0`

All passed:
- zero wall contact events
- minimum clearance ≥ `0.05 m`
- final speed < `0.03 m/s`

### Concise diagnosis
The wall collision was not a MuJoCo or geometry issue; it was a controller design defect. A fixed `0.12 m` trigger is too late and does not generalize across masses or environments. A stopping-distance-based controller fixes that and was independently verified across the provided robot/world variants.
